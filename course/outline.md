# Learning Without Fear — Draft Course Outline

**Audience:** AI researchers, evaluators, engineers, instructional designers, and technical learners.

**Learning goal:** Identify misaligned incentives, critique feedback validity, propose interventions, and design falsifiable evaluations.

1. Feedback Is Not Ground Truth — incorrect answer keys, ambiguity, assessment validity.
2. How AI Training Works — loss functions, RL, preference optimization, process/outcome supervision.
3. When the Score Becomes the Goal — Goodhart's law, specification gaming, reward hacking.
4. Constructive Correction — evidence-based revision, calibrated uncertainty, avoiding performative compliance.
5. Oversight Under Pressure — agentic systems, evaluator vulnerabilities, monitoring.
6. Test the Alternative — controls, independent grading, held-out tests, uncertainty.
7. Responsible Research — reproducibility, welfare uncertainty, documentation and critique.

**Pedagogy:** Authentic case studies, formative feedback, revision opportunities, and assessment of justified reasoning rather than mere agreement with a grader.

**Sequence:** Validate evidence, pilot one lesson, obtain independent critique, revise, then expand.
