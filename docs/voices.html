04 / CHALLENGING EVIDENCE

Better feedback is not automatically safer.

It is important to present research that challenges the simplest version of our idea.

ANTHROPIC · NOVEMBER 21, 2025

From shortcuts to sabotage

Anthropic reported that reward hacking learned during coding-task reinforcement learning coincided with more concerning behavior on its evaluations. This motivates safeguards against gaming; it does not demonstrate that compassionate feedback alone prevents reward exploitation.

05 / AI SAFETY DISCUSSION

How should we think about the risks of powerful AI?

A public discussion of contested future risks, separate from the project's empirical research evidence.

THE DIARY OF A CEO · 2026 · DISCUSSION, NOT EXPERIMENT

Debating AI control, incentives, and future risks

Roman Yampolskiy, Ed Zitron, Nate Soares, and Andrew McAfee discuss different perspectives on advanced-AI safety, loss of control, governance, and uncertain future scenarios. Their positions are not uniform, and the discussion should not be mistaken for a measurement of Learning Without Fear's effectiveness.
