## Scientific guardrails
Preserve the original question: *What if we trained AI the way we teach human children?* Clarify that human learning processes and model optimization are not identical. Preserve the narrower, untested hypothesis about feedback informativeness, evaluator pressure, and truthful failure reporting. Differentiate inference-time changes from weight updates. Treat non-degrading training as a precautionary ethical design commitment, not a demonstrated improvement in model performance or welfare.
